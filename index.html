Welcome to Dooley House

Welcome to the Dooley House, home to four college students and a concerning amount of questionable decisions.

Living with roommates is supposed to be a memorable college experience. Here at Dooley House, that is definitely the case, although not always for the right reasons.

This website takes an inside look at three of the house's most interesting residents: Carlos, Isaac, and Nico. From questionable habits to embarrassing moments, nobody is safe from investigation.

Meet the Suspects

Three roommates. Three personalities. Three very different levels of concern.

The aspiring golf professional who spends more time watching golf videos than actually improving his game. Between the endless Instagram reels and mysterious sleeping schedule, Carlos is an interesting case.

The Roommate Rating System

Each roommate is evaluated based on their habits, cleanliness, personality, and overall contribution to the house.

The final rating is out of 10. These ratings are completely subjective, probably unfair, and absolutely final.
